How it works

Each month the balance is multiplied by (1 + annual return ÷ 12), then the monthly contribution is added. That monthly compounding is why growth accelerates over time.

Worked example (defaults): $10,000 start + $500/month for 20 years at 8% = $343,778 final, on $130,000 contributed.

Frequently asked questions

Is the return guaranteed?

No. The return you enter is an assumption for illustration; real investment returns vary and can be negative.

How often is interest compounded?

Monthly in this calculator: each month the balance grows by the annual rate divided by 12, then the contribution is added.
