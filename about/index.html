What this site is

A collection of everyday calculators: mortgage, loan, investment growth, federal income tax, budgeting, tip, percentage and BMI. Every tool is free, needs no sign-up, and gives an instant result in US dollars.

How accuracy is handled

  • Mortgage and loan payments use the standard amortization formula used by lenders.
  • The tax estimator uses published 2025 IRS federal brackets and standard deductions, plus FICA rates (Social Security 6.2% up to $176,100; Medicare 1.45%).
  • Investment growth uses monthly compounding.
  • BMI uses the standard 703 formula and CDC adult categories.
  • Each calculator page shows its formula and a worked example with the default numbers, so the result can be verified by hand.

What this site is not

Results are estimates for education only. They are not financial, tax or medical advice, and they do not replace a lender quote, a tax professional, or a clinician. See the Disclaimer for details.

Your numbers stay with you

All calculations run in your browser. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
