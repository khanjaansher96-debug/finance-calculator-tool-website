How it works

Taxable income = gross income − pre-tax deductions − the 2025 standard deduction (Single $15,750; Married filing jointly $31,500; Head of household $23,625). Federal tax is then calculated bracket by bracket at 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35% and 37%. Social Security is 6.2% up to the $176,100 wage base; Medicare is 1.45% of all income. State tax is not included. Note on rounding: the exact take-home is $68,348.50, which displays as $68,349.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include state tax?

No. This page covers federal tax, Social Security and Medicare only. State tax depends on where you live.

Why is the marginal rate higher than the effective rate?

Only income inside each bracket is taxed at that bracket's rate, so the average (effective) rate is lower than the top (marginal) rate.
