How it works

Loan = home price − down payment. Monthly principal & interest uses the standard amortisation formula: payment = loan × r ÷ (1 − (1+r)−n), where r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months. Tax and insurance are divided by 12, HOA is added monthly, and PMI at 0.5% of the loan per year applies only when the down payment is under 20% of the price.

Worked example (defaults): $425,000 − $85,000 = $340,000 loan at 6.75% for 30 years gives $2,205 principal & interest, plus $390 tax and $150 insurance = $2,745 per month, with $453,884 total interest.

Frequently asked questions

How is PMI decided?

PMI applies only when the down payment is under 20% of the home price. This page uses 0.5% of the loan per year, divided by 12.

Does the payment include tax and insurance?

Yes. The headline payment is principal & interest plus monthly tax, monthly insurance, HOA and PMI if any.
