Percentage Calculator Guide: Percent Of, Increase, Decrease & Discount Explained

A percentage is simply a part out of 100. If a shop says 20% off, it means $20 off for every $100 of price. Once you know the three basic formulas below, most everyday percentage questions take only seconds.

This guide is general information only, with worked examples you can check by hand.

1. What is a percent of a number?

Formula: (percent ÷ 100) × number.

Example: 20% of $250 = (20 ÷ 100) × 250 = $50. So a 20% tip on a $250 bill is $50, and the total is $300.

Quick check: 10% of a number is the number divided by 10. Double it for 20%, halve it for 5%. For example, 10% of $250 is $25, so 20% is $50.

2. What percent is one number of another?

Formula: (part ÷ whole) × 100.

Example: 45 out of 180 = (45 ÷ 180) × 100 = 25%. This is the same maths used for test scores, completion rates, and how much of a budget has been spent.

3. Percentage increase

Formula: ((new value − old value) ÷ old value) × 100.

Example: a price rises from $80 to $100. The increase is $20, and ($20 ÷ $80) × 100 = 25% increase.

4. Percentage decrease and discounts

Use the same formula, but the result is a fall. Example: a $250 item with 20% off saves $50, so you pay $200. A discount is not the same as a sale tax or a fee added later, so check the final bill before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a percentage and a percentage point?

A rise from 20% to 25% is a 5 percentage point rise, but a 25% relative increase (5 ÷ 20 × 100). News reports and bank offers sometimes mix these up, so check which one is meant.

How do I work out a tip with percentages?

Use the same “percent of” formula: pick 15%, 18% or 20% of the pre-tax bill. Our tipping guide explains the usual US ranges, and the tip calculator splits the total between people.

Can a percentage be over 100%?

Yes, for growth. If a value doubles, that is a 100% increase, and the new value is 200% of the old value. For a share of a whole, such as a test score, the result cannot exceed 100%.
