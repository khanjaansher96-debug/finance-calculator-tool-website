How it works

X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100. Part as a percent = part ÷ whole × 100. Change = (new − old) ÷ |old| × 100.

Worked examples (defaults): 20% of 250 = $50; 45 is 25% of 180; 80 → 100 is +25%.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between percentage points and percent?

A rise from 20% to 25% is 5 percentage points, but a 25% increase relative to 20%.

Can I use it for discounts?

Yes. X% of a price is the discount amount; the remaining amount shown is what you pay.
