How to use these finance calculators

If you are buying a home, start with the Mortgage Calculator: enter the home price, down payment, interest rate and term, and it shows your real monthly payment including property tax, home insurance, HOA dues and PMI when it applies. For example, a $425,000 home with $85,000 down at 6.75% for 30 years works out at $2,745 per month. If you are planning monthly money instead, use the 50/30/20 Budget Calculator: split your take-home pay into 50% needs, 30% wants and 20% savings, and see at a glance where your spending stands against that rule.

For everyday questions, the smaller tools are faster. The Tip Calculator splits a restaurant bill and tip between people in seconds. The Percentage Calculator answers what X% of Y is, what percent one number is of another, and how much a discount saves. The Loan Calculator shows monthly payment, total interest and payoff date for auto, personal and student loans. The Investment Growth Calculator shows how a starting amount plus monthly contributions can grow over 1 to 50 years. The 2025 Income Tax Estimator breaks down federal tax, Social Security, Medicare and take-home pay by filing status. The BMI Calculator uses the standard 703 formula and shows the CDC category and healthy weight range for your height.

Each tool is separate, loads on its own clean URL, and shows its formula with a worked example, so you never have to trust a number blindly. Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere by this site, and every result is an estimate for education only, not financial, tax or medical advice.
