How it works

Tip = bill × tip %. Total = bill + tip. Per person = total ÷ people. Tip per person = tip ÷ people.

Frequently asked questions

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax total?

Custom in the US is usually the pre-tax amount, but tipping on the total is also common.

How is the split calculated?

The total (bill + tip) is divided equally by the number of people.
