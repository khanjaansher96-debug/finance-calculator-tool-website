How it works

BMI = 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (inches)2. CDC adult categories: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy, 25–29.9 overweight, 30+ obese. Healthy range = the weights that give BMI 18.5 to 24.9 at your height.

Worked example (defaults): 5′9″ (69 in), 180 lb → 703 × 180 ÷ 69² = 26.6, Overweight, healthy range 125–169 lb. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. BMI is a screening measure for adults. Muscle, age and health conditions affect what it means for an individual.

Why 703 in the formula?

703 converts pounds and inches to the metric BMI formula (kg/m²) without converting units first.
