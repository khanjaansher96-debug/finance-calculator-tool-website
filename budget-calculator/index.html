How it works

Split take-home pay into 50% needs (housing, food, transport, bills), 30% wants (eating out, subscriptions, fun) and 20% savings/debt payoff. The page compares each of your amounts with its target and shows whether you are over or under.

Worked example (defaults): $5,200 take-home → targets $2,600 / $1,560 / $1,040. With $2,900 / $1,400 / $900 you save 17.3%, $140 a month short of the 20% target.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as needs?

Housing, utilities, groceries, transport, insurance and minimum debt payments — costs you must pay.

What if my needs are over 50%?

That is common in high-cost areas. Treat 50/30/20 as a target to move toward, not a pass/fail test.
